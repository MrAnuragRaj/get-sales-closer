About Revenue Doctor

What Revenue Doctor Analyzes

Revenue Doctor analyzes your entire lead pipeline, conversation quality, response speed, and funnel leaks to identify exactly where revenue is being lost. Most teams discover 2–4 high-impact fixes within the first report.

  • Lead response time patterns and first-touch conversion rates
  • Conversation quality scores across all channels
  • Funnel stage drop-off points and root causes
  • AI automation health and execution failures
  • Channel performance comparison (SMS, WhatsApp, Voice, Messenger)

How It Improves Revenue

  • Pinpoints the single highest-leverage fix for immediate action
  • Provides root-cause analysis — not just surface-level metrics
  • Generates prioritised action plans with effort vs. impact estimates
  • Tracks improvement across report runs (weekly / monthly cadence)

Typical Results

  • Teams using monthly reports see 20–35% improvement in lead-to-appointment rate within 60 days
  • Average of 3 actionable findings per report
  • Each report identifies the top revenue leak causing the most deal loss
  • Most teams recover the cost of their report bundle in the first week of implementing fixes

Reports are private to your organisation. Data is analyzed in-context and not stored by the AI model. Each report credit is non-transferable and non-refundable after generation begins.
